Contribute Instead Of Writing In

Pupil is open source, so the fastest way to fix something is to change it yourself. You do not need permission for any of these:

  • Found a wrong or outdated note? Send the correction and it goes into the next update
  • Found a better open tutorial for a module? Suggest it on the form above
  • Solved a lab project in a cleaner way? Share the repository and we link it as a reference
  • Spotted a broken link on this site? Report it — that is the most useful bug you can send
